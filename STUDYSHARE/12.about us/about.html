ABOUT SHARESTUDY

Learning is better when we share

ShareStudy helps students find, organize, and share useful study materials so everyone can make more of their learning journey.

Our mission

We believe good learning resources should be easier to discover. ShareStudy brings students and study materials together in one place, making it simple to browse notes, past papers, and assignments or contribute resources that may help someone else.
